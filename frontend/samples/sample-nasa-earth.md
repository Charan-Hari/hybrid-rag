# NASA Earth science

This starter brief summarizes the kinds of observations NASA uses to study Earth. NASA missions observe the atmosphere, land, ocean, ice, and living systems from space and from the ground.

## Why observations matter

Long-running observations help scientists understand how Earth systems change over time. Satellite measurements can reveal patterns that are difficult to see from one location, including changes in clouds, vegetation, ocean conditions, and ice.

- Why are long-term measurements useful?
- Which observations can be made from space?
